# Data

`calgary_traffic_incidents_2025.csv`: calendar-2025 subset of the City of Calgary's Traffic Incidents feed, as bundled with Case 5 of the IEEE YP Industry Hackathon 2026.

Source: The City of Calgary, Open Calgary, Traffic Incidents. https://data.calgary.ca/Transportation-Transit/Traffic-Incidents/35ra-9556

Licence: Open Government Licence - City of Calgary.

This is the City's incident feed, not a complete police collision database.
